About IntentGuard

What the system does with your transcript — and how to read what comes back.

How it works

One upload, three steps — nothing happens silently.

  1. 1Extract fields. The system reads product, call date and customer details from the transcript and highlights anything it can’t find — complete those to unlock scoring.
  2. 2Match identity. Exact phone or email hit reuses that MID; no hit assigns a new one. Conflicts stop for human review.
  3. 3Score & save. The baseline model returns a 0–1 interest score with evidence; the event appends to the product’s fact table. History is never rewritten.

An ideal transcript

A short header up top means nothing needs typing — but details buried in the conversation work too.

Product ID: product_b
Product Name: Product B
Date of Call: 2026-10-17
Customer Name: Aarav Sharma
Customer Phone: +91 98765 43210
Customer Email: aarav@example.com
Customer Address: 12 MG Road, Patna
---
Agent: Good morning! Calling about our new savings plan. Would you like the details?
Customer: Yes, I'm interested! Please sign me up and send everything over.
What makes it ideal?
  • All seven fields present as Key: value lines before the ---.
  • Date in YYYY-MM-DD; phone with country code; full email.
  • Dialogue below the header, one speaker per line.
  • Missing a line? The system hunts the conversation for it and asks you to fill the rest.

Model & limits
  • TF-IDF + Logistic Regression baseline, trained on 22 synthetic lines.
  • Scores are raw model outputs, not calibrated probabilities.
  • Explicit opt-outs (“do not call me”…) always resolve to stop.
  • “Not contacted” only ever means no contact history — never a model output.
